title: Product Truth
---

**A shared workspace a small team buys per seat.** Somebody signs up, invites the people
they work with, and the team accrues a bill that grows with the roster.

Two concerns divide the work. **Pricing** answers what a team will pay before they
commit to anything — which is a question people want answered honestly and without
being asked for a credit card first. **Team** is how people get into a workspace and
out of it again, which is where most of the product's real rules live: who may invite,
what happens to somebody invited twice, and what an admin is told when they try.

**What it deliberately does not do:** bill anyone. The estimator quotes and never
charges, and nothing on the pricing side touches money. That boundary is why the
estimator can be shown to somebody who has not signed up.
