---
title: Teamspace
---
A shared workspace a small team buys per seat. Someone signs up, invites the people
they work with, and the team accrues a bill that grows with the roster.

Two concerns divide the work: **pricing**, which answers what a team will pay before
they commit to anything, and **team**, which is how people get in and out of a
workspace. Both are user-facing; what neither owns is the machinery underneath them —
holding an account, sending mail — which lives on the capability side.
