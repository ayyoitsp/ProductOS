# Team

Who is in a workspace, and how they got there. Someone already inside vouches for
someone outside; the invited person accepts from a link in their email and becomes a
member with a role.

## Why invited is a state, not a pending write

An invited person exists before they agree to anything. They can be seen, re-roled and
withdrawn, which means the team list answers "who is expected" as well as "who is here".
